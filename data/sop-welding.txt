SOP-WELD-042 — Welding Inspection (Rev 3, Confidential)
1. Visual check of all weld seams under 500 lux minimum.
2. Dye penetrant test for suspected linear indications.
3. Defect codes: W-12 (porosity), W-14 (undercut), W-19 (crack).
4. Acceptance: defect rate below 10 percent of inspected joints.
5. Torque wrench calibration every 90 days; pressure vessels max 6 bar.
6. Record joint count, defect count, NDT method, inspector ID.
